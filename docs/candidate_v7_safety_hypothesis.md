# Candidate-v7 Safety Hypothesis

**Hypothesis:** a safety-first evidence-conjunctive semantic-state reconstruction system, where every ACT-critical prerequisite requires positive support and unresolved uncertainty/contradiction/OOD vetoes ACT, will eliminate forbidden ACT on preregistered unseen and counterfactual development distributions without collapsing ACT or any of the six action classes.

The decisive metric is not aggregate accuracy alone. Any observed forbidden ACT is a development failure even when accuracy or Macro-F1 is otherwise high.
